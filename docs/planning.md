# Plans, presets, calendar and volume

`state.plans` and how a plan is consumed, presets and their order, the Calendar tab, weeks (`weekMode`), volume and its targets, deloads, and the week strip on Home. Read this whole file before changing anything it describes, and update it in the same commit when that behaviour changes (CLAUDE.md, *Docs*).

- **Volume counts cardio in minutes and everything else in sets, and never adds the two together.** `volumeForWeek` / `volumeInRange` return `{sets, minutes}` per group. A group is free to hold both (declare it cardio and make one lift an exception, or the reverse), and summing them produced "32 sets" out of two sets and half an hour on a bike. The group's own kind picks which figure is THE number (the one a target grades and the one compared with last period), and the other is shown beside it when there is any of it. They also land under the entry's OWN group: filing every cardio entry under the literal key `"Cardio"` put a session done in "Hell" somewhere else entirely.
- **Preset order is user-set, and the pinned strip is a slice of it.** `state.presets` order is the order presets appear in the Library, the picker and on Home. Both lists are rearranged in a *mode* (`ui.presetOrder` on Library → Presets, `ui.pinnedOrder` on Home) rather than by a permanent grip handle, because the cards themselves are what you tap to start a workout and a handle on them would be one mis-tap from starting the wrong day. Home shows `presets.filter(pinned)`, so `reorderPinnedPresets` rewrites **only the slots the pinned presets already occupy** and leaves unpinned ones exactly where they sit, since arranging your home screen must not shuffle the library behind it.
- **Planning is a second ledger that nothing counts.** `state.plans` is `[{id, date, name, entries, createdAt}]`, one dated session you have decided on and not done. Its `entries` are **ordinary entry objects**, the same shape the log uses, which is why the plan builder is not a screen: `renderWorkoutSheet` takes a `draft.planning` flag and the same picker, presets, set list, set editor and "beat last time" card write a plan instead of a day (`commitPlan` vs `commitWorkout`). Two rules make it a plan rather than a log row with a future date, and both matter: **nothing in `state.plans` is ever counted** (not sets, PRs, volume, weeks or charts, and that is the whole point, since a future-dated *log* entry is believed by every calculation downstream), and **a plan may keep entries with no numbers on them** ("Wednesday: these six lifts, weights on the day"), where `commitWorkout` drops blanks. Backing out of a planning sheet saves it; emptying one deletes it, exactly as for a parked day. One plan per date, kept true in `handleBind`'s `draft.date` branch rather than argued about on save.
- **A plan is consumed lift by lift, not by the day.** `plan-start` turns each planned entry into a *blank* entry carrying `e.plan`, a frozen target snapshot (`planTargetOf`: `{sets:[{reps,weight}], unit}`, or `{minutes,intensity}` for cardio), and stamps `draft.planIds` plus, on each entry, a transient `planFrom`/`planIx` back-link that `stripPlanLink` takes off again on the way into the log. The targets ride into the log inside the entries, so "did I do what I said I would" is answerable from `state.log` alone, forever, with no second source of truth about whether a day happened. **Saving a day is not a declaration that the session is over**: people save after the first lift and carry on, so `prunePlans` leaves whatever is still blank *and still in the sheet* on the plan, stamped `startedOn`, and `edit-day` hydrates those back in when the day is reopened; a lift you deleted from the sheet leaves both, because taking it out is the decision not to do it. The plan only disappears once nothing is left of it, and `renderPlanResult` waits for that same moment rather than scoring a session its user is still in the middle of. The back-link is what makes any of this possible: `e.plan` alone cannot tell a planned lift from an ordinary blank, because a plan that named the exercise and left the weights for the day hands over no target at all. `stashDayDraft` carries `planIds`/`planName`, and the back-links ride along on the cloned entries, so parking a session mid-way does not lose the link either. **Planned numbers are never copied into the log**: they appear as *ghost rows* at the end of the set list (`ghostSetRow`), where ✓ (`plan-tick`) logs one exactly as planned and the row itself (`plan-load-set`) opens the set editor with the target loaded. That is the same tap-to-load rule the suggestion card lives under, and it is what buys the smoothness (a session that went to plan is one tap per set) without ever writing a number into the log that a finger did not put there. **Not every `e.plan` came from here**: the entry window can plan its own sets mid-workout (docs/sets.md), writing the same target with no `planFrom`, which is how everything below tells the two apart. Only an entry with a back-link consumes a calendar plan or earns the result sheet.
- **A plan is not a preset, and the difference is load-bearing.** A preset is a bundle of exercises with **no date and no numbers**: the shape of a session you repeat, and what the pinned strip on Home is for. A plan is **one dated session with the weights you are going for**, which is why today's plan gets its own card above the pinned strip and is gone again tomorrow. They compose, and *Add exercise → Presets* inside a planning sheet is the normal way to start one. Don't let either grow into the other.
- **The Calendar tab is where a plan is born and started** (`renderCalendarTab` = month grid → day card → plans list → deload planner → `renderVolume`). Tapping a day now means two things at once: it still picks the period the volume numbers are read over, and it selects `ui.calDay` for **the day card** (`renderDayCard`), the one surface that answers "what is on this day" in the same shape past or future: what you trained, what you planned, the parked draft if there is one, and the one or two things worth doing about it. Which button is the big one follows the calendar, not the feature: *Start* on today or earlier, *Edit plan* on a day you cannot train yet. On the month grid a **solid dot is a group you trained and a hollow one a group you have planned**, with anything already trained dropped from the planned row.
- **Deloads are planned, never inferred.** `state.deloads` is `[{id, start, end}]` with inclusive ISO dates, set by tapping two days on the Calendar tab. `deloadStatus()` decides what the home banner says: nothing, a countdown once the start is within `DELOAD_HEADSUP` days, then a day-N-of-M badge while it runs. The old five-hard-weeks radar is gone, since it was guessing at something only the lifter knows.
- **A week is seven days from your start date, or the last seven days, the user's call.** `settings.weekMode` is `"program"` (default, and what every existing save migrates to) or `"rolling"`, read through `rollingWeeks()`. Program mode is the spreadsheet's: `weekOf()` counts from `settings.startDate` and every seventh day turns the page. Rolling mode replaces that with a *window*, `windowEnding(day)`, for lifters whose split simply comes round in order and for whom a Sunday and a Monday session are the same session. In rolling mode: the Calendar tab reads `ui.volAnchor` (the LAST of its seven days, set by tapping a calendar day, stepped a whole window at a time by the arrows) and sums with `volumeInRange` instead of `volumeForWeek`; the Progress tab's bar chart uses `rollingSetBlocks` (seven-day blocks counting back from today) instead of `weeklyTotals`; and **the program-week chips disappear** from the header, the history day cards and the workout sheet, because in that mode there is no program week to name. `volumeForWeek`/`weekOf` are deliberately kept for program mode rather than expressed as a date range: `weekOf` floors everything before the start date into week 1, and a range would silently drop those rows. **Deloads are untouched by the setting** and must stay that way: they are dates the user put in the calendar, not something derived from a week number.
- **A volume bar is a promise of a target.** In the Calendar tab's volume list a group only gets a progress bar when the user has set a target for it (`state.volumeGoals`). Without one there is no bar and no grading. The old behaviour filled the bar against the biggest group trained that period and flagged others "Low", which made a bar move for reasons that had nothing to do with the lifter, and called a rest day a failure. An untargeted group instead states the one fact available without a plan: how the period compares with the one before it (`vol.more`/`vol.fewer`/`vol.same`). Don't reintroduce a default target.
- **The Calendar tab's month grid** (`renderCalendar`) layers four independent things on each day cell: a tint for the program week whose numbers are shown below (or a `--raise` tint plus a border for the selected `ui.calDay`), a dashed gold band for a deload, solid dots for the muscle groups trained, and hollow dots for the ones a plan has lined up. Calendar maths is all ISO strings (`isoOf`, `monthOf`, `addMonths`, `monthGrid`, `weekRange`) built from local date parts, so a daylight-saving shift can't move a day.
- **THIS WEEK, ON HOME** (`renderWeekStrip` / `weekDays` / `weekStreak`). Seven days of the user's OWN week (a program week from the start date, or the last seven days in rolling mode, the same definition every other count uses), a filled dot per trained day (tap → that day in the log), a dashed ring per planned day, and the streak in weeks. A week still in progress with nothing in it yet does not break the streak. No target and nothing to lose: it counts what the log holds. Hidden until there is anything to show.
